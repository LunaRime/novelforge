Video tutorials

The recordings show the Chinese interface. Follow the English steps below to set up a project, edit a draft, and read it back.

Create a project and organize your notes

  1. Create a project from the welcome screen, name it, and choose a folder.
  2. Open the novel settings from the project outline, add a worldbuilding note, and press Ctrl+S to save before previewing its formatting.
  3. Press Ctrl+K to open the command palette and find the chapter board.

Edit and read a chapter

  1. Expand the drafts section and open a chapter draft.
  2. Drag to select a passage, underline it in the selection toolbar, and press Ctrl+S to save.
  3. Select the book icon at the top right to open reading mode, then use the chapter menu to switch chapters.

Start your novel

Get the Windows installer or portable edition from the latest stable release. The app is available in Simplified Chinese, English, and Russian.
